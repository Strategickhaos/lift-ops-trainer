Stability Triangle

A counterbalanced forklift pivots on three points — both front wheels and the center of the rear axle. The combined center of gravity must stay inside that triangle. Raising and reaching push it forward; turning pushes it sideways.

Adjust load and turn to see the CG move.

Fall Protection — the one people get wrong

Boom lift — full body harness with a lanyard anchored to the manufacturer's anchor point in the platform. Required. A boom can catapult an occupant on a sudden stop or bounce.

Scissor lift — the guardrail system is the fall protection. A harness is not universally required, but follow the manufacturer and your site rule.

Never anchor to an adjacent structure, never climb the rails, and keep both feet on the platform floor.
